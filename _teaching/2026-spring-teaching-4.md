A hands-on workshop on comparative genomics using `Python`. The case study centered on
*Streptomyces albidoflavus* SM254 – a soil bacterium with antifungal activity against
*Pseudogymnoascus destructans*, the causative agent of White-Nose Syndrome in bats.
Participants worked with precomputed `eggNOG-mapper` annotations to build and interpret
KEGG pathway profiles using `KEGGaNOG`.
